Our approach

What a detailed consultation with Dr. Nihal includes

A structured consultation is more than choosing a remedy. It is about understanding the full story, checking safety and deciding what should be tracked over time.

🩺

One-to-One Consultation

Time to discuss your main concern, timeline, previous treatment, reports and goals rather than using a template protocol.

🧬

Whole-History Review

Symptoms are reviewed alongside sleep, stress, diet, triggers, medicines and relevant medical history.

🛡️

Safety Screening

Red flags, current medicines, age, pregnancy status and situations needing in-person or specialist care are considered.

🥗

Lifestyle Context

Where appropriate, practical sleep, activity, diet and trigger-tracking guidance supports the overall plan.

📈

Tracked Progress

Follow-up can use symptom scores, diaries, photos and relevant reports so change is easier to see.

🌐

Online Across India

Consultations are available online. Patients can discuss concerns from home while clear referral boundaries are maintained.
